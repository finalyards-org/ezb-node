## BDFL: Biodiversity Funding License

For commercial use, even providing a service where the `esp-zb` is used as a software component, non-modified or modified, a commercial license needs to be acquired.

We'll make a separate license agreement every time, but in short it consists of:

- paying 10..20% (default 15%) of your commercial turnover (not profit), due to a service based on this code, to a biodiversity / rewilding project.

	You may suggest a project that you prefer (they may be local), however they must be accepted by the copyright holder. If you don't point out a favoured project, you will be provided some.

- payments go directly from you to the biodiversity/rewilding project.
- you report turnover (of services involving this code) and payments, every 6 months, after the payments have been made
- you allow the copyright holder access to your bookkeeping, to double check reported numbers

	The copyright holder keeps information they are shared with confidential, unless it were to breach this agreement.

- If you deliver a *physical product* with this source included), the rate is double (20..40%, default 30%) of the turnover.

- You agree that the list of funded projects, and their *cumulative* funding (where your contribution is not differentiated) can be publicized.

	We try to ensure that an individual license holder's turnover would not be deducible from such publication.

---

The copyright owner themselves aim to follow these same terms.

The terms are intended to provide a level playing field between the copyright owner, and other companies potentially selling services based on the software. 

A list of projects funded, and the cumulative amounts they have received, can be tracked on the copyright owner's web site. `http://finalyards.xyz`
